Infosphere is the campus guide on the kiosk and on this website. It only keeps what it needs to answer better, never sells anything, and never knows who you are unless you tell us in a feedback note.

What we keep

The questions you ask
The question, the answer Kaela gave, the language and the time. They are not linked to you. The team reads them to find questions Kaela could not answer and to teach her better ones.
Your voice, briefly
When you hold the mic, the recording goes to the Infosphere server, is turned into text there, and is deleted a few seconds later. Only the text is kept, as above.
Page visits
Which page was opened, when, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer) and the site you came from. To count returning visitors we store a short code made from your connection and browser with a secret key. It cannot be turned back into your IP address, and the address itself is never stored.
Feedback you send
Your message, your rating and the page you sent it from. Your name and a way to reach you only if you untick "Send anonymously" and fill them in.

Cookies and storage

One cookie keeps the site secure (it protects the forms from being sent by other sites, and keeps staff signed in to the admin pages). Your browser also remembers whether you picked the light or dark theme. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, which is why there is no cookie banner.

Services the site uses

  • Google Fonts: your browser downloads the typefaces from Google, so Google sees your IP address, like any website you load.
  • Microsoft text-to-speech: some spoken answers are made by sending the answer text to Microsoft's voice service. Only Kaela's answer is sent, nothing about you.
  • Open-Meteo: the weather is fetched by our server, not your browser. Nothing about you is sent.

Why we use it

To answer your questions, to find the questions Kaela could not answer and teach her better ones, to count how many people use the guide, and to read and act on your feedback. Nothing is used for advertising or profiling.

Who can see it

Only the Infosphere team, through password-protected admin pages. We do not sell, rent or share it with anyone.

How we protect it

  • The site only works over an encrypted connection (HTTPS).
  • The admin pages sit at a private address, need a password, and sign staff out when idle. The password itself is never stored, only a scrambled form of it.
  • Visitors are counted with a one-way code, so the stored data does not contain IP addresses.
  • Forms are protected against spam and against being sent from other sites.

Where it is stored

On the server that runs Infosphere, in a database only the Infosphere team can open.

How long we keep it

Until the team deletes it. If you sent feedback and want it removed, ask us on the feedback page; tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said so we can find it.

Your rights

Under Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi) you can ask to see, correct or delete personal data you gave us, such as your name or contact in a feedback note. Send the request through the feedback page and leave a way to reach you. Because questions and visits are not linked to anyone, we cannot pick out yours from them.

You can also choose how much to share:

  • Type instead of talking if you would rather not use your voice.
  • Leave "Send anonymously" ticked when you give feedback.
  • Ask us to delete feedback you sent.

Children

Infosphere has no accounts and does not ask for anyone's age. Children can use the guide like anyone else; we only hold a name or contact if someone types it into a feedback note, and we will delete it on request.

Changes

If what the site keeps changes, this page changes with it and the date at the top moves.

Infosphere is a free guide that helps you find rooms, offices and facilities on campus. By using the kiosk or this website you agree to these simple rules.

Directions are a guide

Kaela's answers come from the campus floor plans and the team's notes. Rooms move, plans change and an area can close before we hear about it, so always follow the signs and the staff on site. In an emergency, use the marked emergency exits and ask security for help; do not rely on the kiosk.

Use it fairly

  • Do not try to break, overload or get around the security of the kiosk or the website.
  • Do not send abusive or harmful messages, or other people's personal details, in feedback.
  • The admin pages are for the Infosphere team only.

Your feedback

When you send feedback, you let the team use it to improve Infosphere, for example by teaching Kaela a better answer. See the privacy policy for what is kept.

No guarantees

Infosphere is provided as it is. Answers are produced automatically and can be wrong, and the service can be unavailable at times, for example during updates. The Infosphere team is not responsible for losses caused by relying on an answer, a route or the service being unavailable.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Republic of Indonesia.

Changes

These terms can change as Infosphere grows; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions? Use the feedback page.
