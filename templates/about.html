Who We Are

We're not an app. We're a voice your campus never had before.

Infosphere started as one question: why does finding a room, an office, or an answer on campus still feel like guesswork? We built the assistant we wished existed.

Scroll

We believe technology on a campus should feel like asking a friend, not filling out a form — fast, honest, and human enough to say "I don't know yet, but I'm learning."

01

We listen first.

Every question — spoken or typed, in Indonesian, English, or Mandarin — starts by actually being heard, not pattern-matched against a script.

02

We understand the space.

Rooms, floors, offices, routes — mapped and cross-referenced for whichever space we're set up to serve, so an answer isn't just correct, it's actually useful to someone standing there.

03

We guide, not just answer.

Every conversation makes the next one sharper. Infosphere is built to keep learning from whichever space it's deployed to — quietly, in the background.

How we think about it

The principles that shape every answer Infosphere gives.
01

Clarity over cleverness

A short, correct answer beats an impressive-sounding vague one, every time.

02

Say "I don't know"

Guessing confidently is worse than admitting a gap — so Infosphere is built to do the latter.

03

Local, not generic

Trained on each space's own data — its rooms, its language, its people — not a one-size-fits-all script.

04

Always improving

Every unanswered question gets reviewed and taught back in — the assistant is never really "finished."
